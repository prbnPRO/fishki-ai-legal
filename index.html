automation tool

Schedule short-form videos without living inside two different apps.

Fishki AI Scheduler is a lightweight publishing tool that takes a finished vertical video, writes a caption with an AI model, and queues it for TikTok and YouTube Shorts — on a schedule you set once.

See how it works →

Why it exists

Publishing the same short video to more than one platform means opening two separate studios, re-typing the same caption twice, and remembering which day each post is supposed to go out. Fishki AI Scheduler removes that busywork: point it at a video file once, and it handles the caption, the hashtags, and the queue.

01

One video, two platforms

The same finished file is prepared for both TikTok and YouTube Shorts, with platform-appropriate captions instead of a copy-pasted one-size-fits-all description.

02

Captions written for you

A short caption and a small, relevant set of hashtags are generated from the video's own script, so nothing goes out blank or generic.

03

A queue, not a to-do list

Videos are scheduled ahead of time on a fixed cadence, so publishing stops being something you have to remember to do manually every few days.

04

Built on official APIs

Publishing happens through the official TikTok Content Posting API and the YouTube Data API — no browser automation, no screen-scraping.

Who this is for

Fishki AI Scheduler is built for TikTok and YouTube creators who publish short-form video to more than one platform and want the scheduling and captioning handled automatically. Any creator managing their own channels can connect their accounts and use it to remove repetitive manual steps from their publishing workflow.
