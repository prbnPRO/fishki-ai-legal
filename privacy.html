Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Fishki AI Scheduler is an automation tool for scheduling and publishing short-form video to TikTok and YouTube. It operates on behalf of the creator who authorizes it, and does not collect or store data about anyone other than the authorizing account holder.

What data is used

The tool works with the following, all of which stay on the operator's own device and accounts:

  • Video files created and edited by the operator, before publishing
  • OAuth access tokens issued by TikTok and Google, used only to publish to the operator's own channels
  • A local queue file recording which videos are scheduled and when

No data about any third party — viewers, followers, or anyone else — is collected, read, or stored by this tool.

How data is used

Video scripts are sent to a third-party AI provider solely to generate a caption and hashtags for that video. Finished videos and their captions are sent to TikTok and YouTube exclusively through each platform's official API, for the sole purpose of publishing them to the operator's own account.

Data sharing

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with any party beyond what is strictly necessary to generate captions and publish videos as described above. There is no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking built into this tool.

Data retention & deletion

OAuth tokens and the local queue file are stored only on the operator's own device and can be deleted at any time by removing the corresponding local files. Revoking access from the TikTok or Google account settings immediately stops the tool from being able to publish.
