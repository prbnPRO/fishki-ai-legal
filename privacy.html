Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Fishki AI Scheduler ("the app") is a personal, single-user tool used by its sole owner to schedule and publish short-form video content to one TikTok account (@fishki_ii). This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data the app accesses

The app uses the TikTok Content Posting API to publish video content on behalf of the account owner. It accesses only:

  • The OAuth access token and refresh token issued by TikTok to authorize publishing on the owner's own account.
  • Basic creator info required by the API (available privacy levels, allowed video duration) needed to publish correctly.

Data the app does not access

The app does not access, collect, or store any data belonging to other TikTok users. It does not read followers, comments, messages, or any content other than the videos the owner explicitly uploads through it.

Data storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own device and are never transmitted to or stored on any third-party server. No user data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Third parties

The app does not share any data with third parties. It communicates only with TikTok's official API endpoints.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the app owner via the TikTok account @fishki_ii.
